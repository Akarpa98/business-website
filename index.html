Our Services

K Abroad provides practical, structured support for individuals navigating life across borders.

We offer holistic advice on selecting the most suitable international arrangement for your personal, legal, and financial situation, before formal residency, tax, or administrative processes begin.

Ideal for individuals who:

  • Want to relocate internationally with complex cross-border considerations
  • Want to relocate due to unstable or conflict-affected environments
  • Work remotely and need a stable, legal and tax base

How We Support You

1. Assessment & Strategy

We assess residency and migration options across countries to identify the most practical, cost-effective pathways.

2. Relocation Planning & Implementation

We design a step-by-step relocation roadmap covering timelines, documentation, and execution support.

3. International Work & Tax Structuring

We help with structuring cross-border freelancing and international income flows.

4. Legal & Administrative Setup

We support with paperwork for the setup of residency, taxation, and social security across jurisdictions.

Services can be delivered as a one-off consultation, a structured relocation plan, or ongoing support. Each engagement starts with an initial consultation to assess your circumstances, goals, budget, and preferred destinations, then a tailored step-by-step approach is developed.
